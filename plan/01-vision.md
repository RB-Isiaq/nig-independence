# 01 — Vision

## One-liner
An immersive, highly animated, **evergreen** website that celebrates Nigeria's Independence
(1 October 1960) every year — always showing the correct anniversary, accurate history and
real data, without yearly code changes.

## Audience
- Nigerians at home and in the diaspora (primary) — mostly **mobile**, often mid-range Android, often metered data.
- Students / teachers looking for a quick, credible history refresher.
- Social sharers — the site should produce a great link preview every year.

## Goals
1. **Correct every year automatically** — anniversary number, ordinal, countdown, "Happy Nth" mode on the day.
2. **Credible** — every claim sourced; data shows its "as of" year.
3. **Delightful** — premium scroll-driven motion in the spirit of nlightmedia.com / neusix.com / arbittechnologyltd.com (sliders, Lottie, glassy visuals, scroll reveals) — but lighter and faster.
4. **Shareable** — dynamic OG image with the current anniversary.
5. **Fast on a slow phone** — see budgets in [04](04-design-and-motion.md).

## Non-goals (for now)
- User accounts, CMS, comments/wish wall (Phase 4, needs moderation).
- Political commentary. We present facts neutrally.

## Success criteria
- On 1 Oct of *any* future year the hero reads "Happy {N}{ordinal} Independence Day" with **zero** deploys.
- Lighthouse mobile: Performance ≥ 85, Accessibility ≥ 95.
- No unsourced fact on the page.
